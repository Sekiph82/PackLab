# PL-0403 — Validation Criteria V01

1. Inventory evidence must come from authorized real source records or an observed physical stocktake; do not infer a list from task examples or synthetic dataset fixtures.
2. Record actual item identity/type/count/location/condition and source/owner provenance only when evidence exists; keep uncertain fields explicitly unknown.
3. If no such inventory or physical samples exist, document the precise blocker and required evidence, leave the task blocked/open, and do not fabricate any list or complete it from planning assumptions.
4. Do not ask the owner for data or physical samples; continue independent tasks that can proceed without the inventory.
5. Preserve privacy and supplier confidentiality; no real inventory is added to a public fixture.
