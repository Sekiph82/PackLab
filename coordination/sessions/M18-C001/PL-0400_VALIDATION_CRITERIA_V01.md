# PL-0400 — Validation Criteria V01

1. Print-ready checklist has pre-capture, sample/label inspection, station/camera/light record, capture sequence, post-capture QA and disposition/signoff sections with useful blank fields.
2. Cleaning is not automatic: no contact-cleaning, label removal, wiping, solvents, filled-package opening or other alteration without object-owner-approved method and use/material checks. Stop all capture for leaks, unknown contents/residue, damage that threatens safe handling or label integrity, contamination, unsafe handling or unknown-use risk. Loose labels require owner disposition before handling; non-contact exterior imaging may proceed only with specific owner/site authorization. Unknown material blocks cleaning/treatment and requires owner/site safety authorization before capture; safe non-contact exterior imaging may proceed if that review permits.
3. Preserve label/artwork; capture it in situ and do not put temporary targets over it. If an owner-approved cleaning/alteration exception exists, record its separate authorization and before/after imagery.
4. Match PL-0396 envelope and PL-0397 proposed station setup; identify limits and actual values as blanks, never present proposed positions as measured. Require scale/calibration provenance without treating a printed target as certified metrology.
5. For standard COLMAP set, keep object orientation fixed and move camera among overlapping viewpoints; use similar illumination within one set. Record rotated-object, polarization and treatment acquisitions separately unless exact alternate lane is validated.
6. Explicitly try/record non-contact reflection options first (diffusion/light position, matte background, viewpoint and exposure), offer PL-0398 paired optional cross-polarization, keep strict PL-0399 treatment gate, and include retry/stop conditions plus missing-view/blur/exposure/background checks.
7. Checklist and evidence do not claim Kenya-site, physical, dimensional accuracy, repeatability or software execution. No test needed for documentation; report milestone test deferral.
8. Include source/provenance and privacy/safety review; independent advisory reviews inspect actual content; record exact no-test rationale and `LOCAL_COMPLETE_AUTONOMOUS_QUEUE_ARMED` only if all criteria are met.
