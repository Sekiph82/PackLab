# PL-0417 — Validation Criteria V01

1. A geometry change requires a new capture, a new Scan Master and a new Design Model revision, with a new asset revision. Prior geometry revisions are retained and never edited.
2. Same-line versus different-package classification follows the contract: a SKU cannot move to a different asset identity.
3. SKUs never move implicitly. Migration is an explicit, reviewed SKU revision.
4. Downstream dependants (label zones, interfaces, drawings) are re-reviewed. Scale and mould-use authority are never upgraded.
5. A synthetic dry-run is provided. No mould, Kenya or physical evidence is claimed.
