# PL-0387 — Validation Criteria V01

1. Use real reconstructed package geometry and independent physical reference measurements.
2. Record method, instrument/calibration, nominal dimensions, observed errors and predeclared tolerance.
3. Results meet the documented tolerance without retuning after measurement.
4. Synthetic/YCB dimensions are not called independent physical truth.
5. Missing physical objects or measurement equipment leaves task open with exact blocker.
